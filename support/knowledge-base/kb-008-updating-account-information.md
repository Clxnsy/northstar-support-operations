# KB-008: Reviewing Account Invoices & Payment History

## Overview
This article guides Billing Contacts and Account Admins on viewing past invoices, managing payment methods, updating sales tax exemption certificates, and understanding user seat licensing charges.

---

## Accessing Your Billing Dashboard
1. Log into Northstar as a **Billing Contact** or **SaaS Admin**.
2. Navigate to `Admin Console -> Billing & Subscription`.
3. View your current **Monthly Recurring Revenue (MRR)** summary, active user seat usage, and upcoming renewal date.

---

## Downloading Invoices
1. Under the **Invoice History** section, locate the target statement.
2. Click **Download PDF** to retrieve an itemized invoice detailing platform base tier fees, additional seat licenses, and state sales tax (if applicable).

---

## Applying Sales Tax Exemption
If your organization holds a valid state sales tax exemption certificate:
1. Email your tax exemption documentation (`Form ST-10` or state equivalent) to `billing@northstar.com`.
2. Our finance team will update your account profile `is_tax_exempt` flag and re-issue tax-free invoices.
